how we work

01find

Find where it lives. We map how your operations really run, across the spreadsheets, inboxes, group chats and half-finished apps. Then we find where the data leaks: the manual re-typing, the copy-paste, the steps only one person knows.

02build

Build one tool per job. Each job gets one tool, wired into a single workflow, built from proven parts rather than a rebuild of what already works. The tool fits your process, not the other way around. Everything gets a fallback, so nothing rests on a single point of failure.

03prove

Prove it on your numbers. We run it against your real data, not a demo, so you see it working before anyone depends on it. If a piece doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.

04hand over

Hand it over, and leave. The whole system becomes yours to run: documented, owned by your team, no lock-in.
